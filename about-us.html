About us


Welcome to Luxe Ultimatum – Nepal’s First & Largest Pre-Owned Automobile Marketplace. At Luxe Ultimatum, we redefine the way Nepal buys and sells pre-owned vehicles. Established with a vision to bring trust, transparency, and quality to the second-hand automobile market, Luxe Ultimatum is proud to be the first and largest second-hand automobile seller in Nepal. We are more than just a dealership — we are a movement. A movement to empower Nepali drivers with smarter, safer, and more affordable choices when it comes to owning a vehicle.

Our Mission


At Luxe Ultimatum, our mission is to revolutionize the second-hand automobile industry in Nepal by delivering unmatched trust, transparency, and value to every customer. We are committed to making reliable vehicles accessible and affordable for all, without compromising on quality. Through our rigorous inspection processes, fair pricing, and customer-first approach, we aim to provide a seamless and stress-free car buying and selling experience. With a growing network of branches across the country, we strive to bring convenience and personalized service to every corner of Nepal. By combining innovation with integrity, we empower individuals to make smarter vehicle decisions while building long-term relationships grounded in honesty, service, and satisfaction.

Our Journey


Luxe Ultimatum’s story began in a small garage, where three high school dropouts — Astitwa Mohan Bhattarai, Prasin Bohara, and Saksham Joshi — dared to dream big and turned their childhood plan into reality: building the largest automobile reseller in Nepal. Despite lacking formal educational credentials, they shared a bold vision to revolutionize Nepal’s pre-owned automobile market.

In a market often clouded by uncertainty and skepticism, these three friends saw an opportunity to bring trust, transparency, and quality to vehicle buyers and sellers across Nepal. With limited resources but an abundance of grit, they began buying old, overlooked cars, refurbishing them with care, and offering them to customers at fair prices. Their commitment to honest dealings and customer satisfaction quickly earned them respect and loyalty.

What began as a small-scale operation in an old garage soon gained momentum. Through tireless effort, countless late nights, and a relentless focus on customer needs, Luxe Ultimatum transformed from a humble startup into a pioneering business. Today, it proudly holds the title of Nepal’s first and largest second-hand automobile marketplace. More than just a dealership, Luxe Ultimatum has become a movement, empowering Nepali drivers with smarter, safer, and more affordable choices when it comes to owning a vehicle.

The founders didn't only build their dream, they built the legacy

Our Vision


To be Nepal’s most trusted and innovative automobile marketplace — revolutionizing the pre-owned vehicle industry by delivering unmatched quality, transparency, and customer empowerment.
