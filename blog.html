Luxe Ultimatum Officially Recognized as Nepal’s Largest Second-Hand Automobile Dealer

  • 📍 Kathmandu |
  • posted by BBC Nepal
  • 1 comment
  • 13 June, 2023

Luxe Ultimatum, Nepal’s pioneering pre-owned vehicle dealership, has officially been recognized as the largest second-hand automobile dealer in the country, marking a significant milestone for both the company and the nation’s growing used vehicle market.
Founded by three determined entrepreneurs — Astitwa Mohan Bhattarai, Prasin Bohara, and Saksham Joshi — Luxe Ultimatum began as a small-scale operation in a Kathmandu garage. Today, it has grown into a national brand with an extensive network of branches, a strong digital presence, and a loyal customer base that spans across Nepal.

🚘 A New Standard for Pre-Owned Vehicles in Nepal
The pre-owned automobile market in Nepal has long faced challenges — from lack of transparency and trust to the absence of standardized pricing and inspection procedures. Luxe Ultimatum has turned these pain points into opportunities by building a system that prioritizes honesty, reliability, and quality assurance.
With a growing inventory of certified pre-owned cars and motorcycles, Luxe Ultimatum ensures that every vehicle goes through a rigorous multi-point inspection process before being listed for sale. Customers are provided with detailed inspection reports, transparent pricing, financing options, and warranty packages, making vehicle ownership more accessible and risk-free than ever before.

🔧Humble Beginnings, Bold Vision
Founded by three high school dropouts Luxe Ultimatum began as a small-scale operation in a local garage. With minimal capital but an unshakable belief in their vision, the trio started buying old and overlooked vehicles, refurbishing them with care, and reselling them at fair prices.
What set them apart was their commitment to customer-first service, honest dealings, and long-term value — rare traits in an unregulated second-hand market. Word of mouth spread quickly, and their once-small business began to gain traction, attracting buyers from across Nepal and beyond.
From day one, Luxe Ultimatum positioned itself not just as a dealership but as a movement — a movement to empower Nepali drivers with smarter, safer, and more affordable vehicle choices.

🛠️ Built on Trust, Backed by Service
What truly differentiates Luxe Ultimatum is its after-sales ecosystem. Buyers aren’t left on their own after a purchase. The dealership offers:

  • Extended warranty options
  • On-site and mobile service centers
  • Customer support and maintenance plans
  • Buy-back and resale options
This holistic approach has helped Luxe Ultimatum maintain one of the highest customer retention rates in the industry, with many customers returning for upgrades or recommending the platform to friends and family.

🏁 A Legacy in Motion
What started as a dream shared by three young friends is now a reality that’s changing the way an entire nation buys and sells vehicles. Luxe Ultimatum’s journey is a powerful testament to what can happen when vision, integrity, and innovation come together.
As Luxe Ultimatum celebrates this major milestone, one thing is clear:
This is just the beginning.
